Cross-modal attacks

Push pixels until CLIP buys your caption. Tab two runs the paper CNN track on MNIST / CIFAR-10.

Caption It is the text you want the image to “look like” to CLIP. The attack nudges pixels so the model’s image–text score for this exact sentence goes up — even if the picture still looks like a dog or a car to you. It is the goal of the cross-modal attack, not a label of what is really in the photo.
Epsilon It is the max strength of each pixel tweak (in image units 0–1). Smaller ε → subtler noise, harder for the attack to push similarity to your caption. Larger ε → stronger attack, but the image may look more distorted. Leave blank to use the project default.
